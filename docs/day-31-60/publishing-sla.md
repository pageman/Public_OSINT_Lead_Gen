# Publishing SLA

## Cadence

- Monday: publish weekly pulse
- Tuesday: distribute on LinkedIn, X, and email
- Wednesday: review replies and booked calls
- Thursday: refresh source review and data quality
- Friday: backlog grooming and next-week framing

## Roles

- Founder: final narrative sign-off
- Analyst: evidence review and source traceability
- Builder: data refresh and route integrity
- Ops: lead follow-up and meeting routing

## Service Levels

- weekly pulse published by Monday noon local time
- high-intent inbound lead reviewed same day
- insights form submissions scored immediately
