# Nurture Sequence

## Email 1: Immediate Follow-Up

Subject: Your fintech operations pulse is ready

Purpose:

- confirm submission
- reinforce the strongest current signal
- direct to briefing CTA if urgency is high

## Email 2: 48-72 Hours Later

Subject: Where the hidden cost usually lands first

Purpose:

- explain why queue pain often appears before visible outages
- surface one real operator use case
- invite reply with current workflow bottleneck

## Email 3: 5-7 Days Later

Subject: If one queue is already expensive, start there

Purpose:

- shift from education to execution
- frame the paid pilot as a narrow sprint around one queue or corridor
- direct to pilot intake
