# Gated Insights Specification

## Goal

Convert public interest into qualified lead capture with a small number of clearly valuable unlocked assets.

## Assets

1. Historical friction export
   - CSV of corridor, compliance, and platform-fragility observations
2. 7-day nowcast
   - concise forecast of where operational pain is likely to rise next
3. Operator briefing deck
   - short internal-ready summary for ops and risk leads

## Unlock Fields

- work email
- company
- role
- urgency
- current pain

## Post-Submit Behavior

- calculate lead score
- route to success page
- recommend booking a briefing if score is high
- persist or forward lead to CRM/webhook

## Success Criteria

- form completion rate is measurable
- lead score is attached to each submission
- the buyer sees a clear next step after form completion
