# Pilot Onboarding Checklist

## Before Kickoff

- identify one queue or corridor to focus on
- confirm primary operator owner
- confirm weekly review cadence
- gather current backlog and aging metrics
- confirm success metric for the sprint

## During Week 1

- map intake and handoff points
- identify repeated exception patterns
- identify high-friction partner or corridor interactions
- define first-wave prioritization rules

## During Weeks 2-4

- track queue aging change
- track manual rework
- review escalation path quality
- capture quick wins and blocked items
