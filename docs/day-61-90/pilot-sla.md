# Pilot SLA

## Response Expectations

- inbound pilot intake reviewed within 1 business day
- kickoff scheduled within 5 business days of acceptance
- weekly operating review delivered on agreed cadence

## Client Inputs Required

- named owner
- workflow pain statement
- current-state queue metrics
- access to policy, escalation, or support context needed for the sprint

## Success Measurement

- reduced queue aging
- reduced manual touches
- better prioritization quality
- improved escalation clarity
