# 5-Minute Sales Call Script

Date: April 2, 2026

## Minute 0-1: Opening

Thanks for taking the call. The short version is that we track public signals that tend to create cross-border payments operations pain before the issue becomes an obvious outage, and we use that signal to help teams fix one high-friction workflow fast.

## Minute 1-2: Problem

Most teams already see the news and status pages. What they usually do not have is a clean view into:

- which policy or sanctions changes are likely to increase manual review load
- which partner or corridor combinations are becoming brittle
- where compliance, fraud, and exception handling are colliding into one manual queue

That is where the real operating cost usually shows up first.

## Minute 2-3: Current Alpha

As of April 2, 2026, the strongest public signals we see are:

- elevated regulatory and policy change density
- mixed platform brittleness beneath otherwise stable public uptime
- rising queue-collision risk between compliance and exception handling

The point is not to predict headlines. The point is to know where backlog, retries, and reconciliation work are most likely to increase next.

## Minute 3-4: Offer

We give away the public signal for free.

When the signal exposes a real operational bottleneck, we offer a focused 30-day sprint around one queue or one corridor. That usually covers:

- triage logic
- escalation mapping
- manual-review prioritization
- reconciliation and exception handling support

## Minute 4-5: Qualification

Ask:

1. Which queue or corridor is creating the most avoidable manual work right now?
2. Is the pain showing up as compliance backlog, payout retries, reconciliation effort, or support volume?
3. Do you need help this week, this month, or are you still mapping the problem?

## Close

If there is one queue you already know is expensive and noisy, that is the best place to start. We can use the public signal to frame it, then turn it into a short execution sprint rather than a long platform project.
