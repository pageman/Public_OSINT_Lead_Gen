# Fintech Alpha ICP Brief

## Wedge

- Category: B2B fintech operations intelligence
- Initial wedge: cross-border payments disruption and compliance friction
- Buyer persona: heads of payments operations, risk operations leads, and COOs at cross-border fintechs, PSPs, and remittance platforms

## Problem

These teams operate in a constant fog of:

- sanctions and regulatory changes
- payout rail instability
- bank partner interruptions
- fraud spikes tied to corridor or merchant shifts
- manual backlog in compliance, reconciliation, and exception handling

Most teams do not lack raw news. They lack a clean operational read on what matters now, what is noise, and which workflows will break first.

## Public Signal Thesis

The free surface should answer:

- which corridors are getting harder to operate this week
- which rule changes are likely to increase manual review load
- which counterparties, rails, or geographies are becoming operationally brittle
- which signals are moving before dashboards inside most fintechs catch up

## Conversion Promise

Use the public signal for free. Pay only when the signal needs to become:

- triage rules
- workflow automation
- reconciliation support
- compliance exception handling
- surge operations capacity

## Why This Wedge Works

- Public inputs are abundant.
- Operational interpretation is scarce.
- The gap between awareness and execution is large.
- The buyer already feels the pain in staffing, backlog, false positives, payout failures, and compliance overhead.

## Day 0-30 Success Criteria

- launch a public “Cross-Border Fintech Operations Pulse”
- publish 3 updates
- define 10-20 high-value sources
- create a direct CTA for briefing or pilot demand
- identify the first repeatable paid workflow behind the public signal
