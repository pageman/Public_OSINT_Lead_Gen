# Fintech Alpha Editorial QA

## Before Publishing

- Does every major claim have at least 2 sources?
- Does every recommendation have at least 1 high-confidence source?
- Are we explicit about what is observation versus forecast?
- Are we describing operator pain rather than generic market commentary?
- Is the update readable in under 3 minutes?
- Is there one clear takeaway rather than too many weak points?
- Is the CTA tied to execution support rather than vague interest?

## Do Not Publish

- unsupported claims about fraud levels
- corridor recommendations based only on social chatter
- legal or compliance advice framed as definitive guidance
- statements that imply proprietary customer knowledge when only public data is used
