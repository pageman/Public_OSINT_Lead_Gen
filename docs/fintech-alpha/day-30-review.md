# Fintech Alpha Day-30 Review

## Goal

Decide whether to move this wedge into day 31-60 gated insight buildout.

## Metrics To Review

- site sessions for fintech pages
- repeat visitors from target firms
- email captures or briefing requests
- update clickthrough rate
- replies from target operators
- number of conversations that mention operational usefulness

## Go Criteria

- at least one target account asks for help beyond the free content
- public updates produce replies or meetings from real operators
- readers mention corridor, compliance, payout, or queue pain rather than generic strategy
- the team can produce the weekly update without excessive manual research overhead

## No-Go Criteria

- interest is broad but non-operational
- engagement comes mainly from consultants or observers rather than buyers
- the updates require too much unsupported inference to stay useful
- no clear execution service emerges behind the signal

## Day 31-60 Build If Positive

- gated export of corridor and compliance friction observations
- lead form with company, role, and urgency
- lead scoring tied to real workflow pain
- post-submit briefing flow
